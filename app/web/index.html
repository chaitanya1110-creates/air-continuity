iPhone Screen Mirroring

Mirror your screen natively via AirPlay — no app install needed

Use Native AirPlay Screen Mirroring
📺 How to Mirror Your iPhone Screen:
① On your Windows PC, click Start AirPlay Receiver in the AirContinuity app.
② On this iPhone, swipe down from the top-right corner to open Control Center.
③ Tap the Screen Mirroring button (two overlapping rectangles icon).
④ Select AirContinuity from the device list.
⑤ Your iPhone screen will appear on your Windows PC!
💡 Tip
Make sure both your iPhone and Windows PC are connected to the same Wi-Fi network. The AirPlay receiver must be started on the PC before you can discover it.

AirPlay screen mirroring uses Apple's native protocol for the best quality and lowest latency. No browser-based capture needed.
